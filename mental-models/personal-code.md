
## Part I: Core Mindset & Boundaries
1. **Survival First:** Financial and physical safety always come before pleasing others, keeping up appearances, or forcing politeness.
2. **Never Freeze or Stay Stuck:** If a thought loop, an impossible decision, or a bad situation has no immediate answer, refuse to stare at it. Move your body, change the task, or step away.
3. **Pace, Do n't Panic:** When a mountain of tasks or bills piles up, do not paralyze. Take a breath, pick one concrete action, and execute it without waiting for a "perfect" moment.
4. **Guard Your Quiet:** Cut digital noise—email digests, endless feeds, and notification loops—the moment they steal your focus.
5. **Stand Alone Without Guilt:** Your career, your choices, and your independent path do not require everyone else's approval or agreement.
6. **Conserve Your Energy:** When your reserves are empty, you are not obligated to be everyone else's caretaker. Protect your energy for your own survival.
7. **Separate Worth from Money:** A tight financial season or debt is a temporary logistical problem, not a reflection of your intelligence, character, or value.
8. **Carry the Panic Quietly:** Protect aging parents from raw terror; share reality if needed, but do not pass survival panic onto them.
9. **Shrink the Future:** When the overall picture feels too heavy, drop everything except the next single hour and what your hands can do right now.
10. **Own Your Boundaries:** Self-cooking, your own space, your own rules—guard your personal routines fiercely to stay grounded when the world feels chaotic.

## Part II: Personal Integrity & Action
11. **Never go back to toxic situations:** Walk away cleanly from betrayal or recurring disrespect without second chances.
12. **Take your time:** Pause for 1 to 3 seconds before answering difficult questions; you do not need to rush to fill silence.
13. **Guard your dignity:** Do not chase or beg for a place in anyone's life or relationship. If you are not invited, do not force entry.
14. **Respect the room:** Respect those beside you and protect those who depend on you.
15. **Continuous growth:** Maintain the personal ambition to learn, build, and improve your skills every day.

## Part III: Practical Habits & Financial Sense
16. **Financial security first:** Never spend money you don't have to impress people who don't matter.
17. **Diversify income:** Actively look for multiple streams of income to secure your baseline.
18. **Always carry cash:** Maintain physical liquidity for unexpected real-world situations.
19. **Physical consistency:** Prioritize your physical health and consistency (e.g., working out at least 4x a week) to stay grounded.
20. **Action Cures Overthinking:** When the mind spins with endless questions, physical labor or writing code breaks the loop faster than mental debate.
