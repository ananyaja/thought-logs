# Reflections & Journal

A public collection of notes, mental models, and reflections documenting a transition away from endless theoretical preparation and toward hands-on, shared creation. 

## What This Is
* **A Living Record:** Capturing lessons learned along the way—shifting from solitary studying to building practical projects out in the open.
* **A Digital Garden:** A space that evolves continuously as new insights emerge, old patterns are examined, and understanding deepens.

## Structure
* `journal/` — Personal notes on moving from theory to practice and shedding the weight of the endless race.
* `mental-models/` — Core realizations on isolation, shared knowledge, and building with intention.
